# Rollback matrix

Rollback means restoring safe service, not rewriting history.  Every target command has a correlation/idempotency key and audit record.  Where an external or governance effect is final, the corrective action is pause plus reconciliation or a governed compensating transition.

|Slice|Rollback deadline|Data that can be reverted|Data that cannot be reverted|External effects|Reconciliation requirements|User communication requirements|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|S1 public reads|Any time before old removal|Routing, cached/projection snapshot, asset release|Publicly observed content cannot be “unseen”|Browser cache/CDN content|Compare snapshot/projection version and row counts/hashes|Status notice only if public views were materially incorrect|
|S2 account/profile|Before first target-only mutation per account; otherwise immediate pause|Unpublished profile projection and uncommitted claim|A principal claim, tombstone, and audit record require controlled correction|Off-chain erasure request may already be queued|Account-level version/correlation reconciliation; decide target-only mutations individually|Direct notice to affected account owners, especially tombstone/consent cases|
|S3 identity/KYC|During announced overlap; no rollback of invalidated secrets|Provider routing and unaccepted attestations|Expired/revoked tokens and invalidated sessions; issued attestation audit|Email/OAuth/KYC provider interactions|Provider case/attestation status and webhook event ID reconciliation|Advance notice of re-login; direct notice for a verification interruption|
|S4 assessment|Until accepted result is used by governance/payment|Pending job and unaccepted run|Accepted result/audit; external AI request/log may already exist|AI/provider cost, notifications, private evidence|Run/job IDs, accepted-result versions, retry state|Notify users whose evaluation status or retry availability changed|
|S5 governance|Before period finalization; after that only governed appeal|Open target period and unfinalized votes under approved policy|Final outcome, hold transition, audit, and any public certified result|Scheduled notifications/maintenance|Vote totals, eligibility snapshot, period clock, hold state; record appeal decision|Public incident notice for an incorrect outcome; direct notice to held/affected users|
|S6 policy/reserves|Before first obligation under policy; otherwise pause immediately|Inactive policy/observation and draft reserve values|Activated policy/audit and any obligation created under it|Oracle service polling|Policy version, oracle source/time, reserve/external balance reconciliation|Governance/operator notice; public status if displayed policy was wrong|
|S7 ICP/ICRC payments|Until ledger call; after call, until reconciliation declares settlement|Unclaimed/authorized obligation can return to `pending` or `reconcile_required`|A ledger transfer and settled obligation cannot be reversed unilaterally|Ledger transfer, fee, finality/reorg handling|Memo/block index, ledger balance, reserve, entitlement and user history; no resend while ambiguous|Immediate direct notice for delayed/ambiguous/incorrect payment; public incident status if systemic|
|S8 external chains|Until executor broadcast; after broadcast, until finality policy closes|Unsigned instruction or unbroadcast obligation|Broadcast transfer, gas fee, chain receipt, final settlement|HSM/KMS signing, RPC broadcast/finality/reorg|Instruction signature, executor log, independent chain lookup, recipient/reserve balance|Direct notice for affected payouts and chain-specific incident/status update|
|S9 retirement|Until archive retention/purge decision is executed|Endpoint routing and read-only archive access|Purged data, rotated credentials, target financial/governance history|Archive storage/deletion providers|Archive checksum, restore drill, retention certificate, no-writer verification|Advance deprecation notice and support path; targeted notice if access/export changes|
